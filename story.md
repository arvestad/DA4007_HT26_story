# A DA4007 fairytale

Once upon a time, there was a castle by a lake called Mälaren.
The castle was made by green bricks and they were magical --- the castle would actually grow! They were forged from Mälarens' stones touched by northern lights. And what everyone thought was a tower, was actually a beanstalk!
At the top of the beanstalk, lived a lonely giant named Sven. Like me, he didn't understand Git; but he was very resilient and continued to practice day after day, until he was able to track all the magic castle modifications. One day, after practicing for so long, he was finally able to create a book where he had all his magic bricks and walls documented.
