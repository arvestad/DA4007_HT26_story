# A DA4007 fairytale

Once upon a time, there was a castle by a lake called Mälaren.
The castle was made by green bricks and they were magical --- the castle would actually grow! They were forged from Mälarens' stones touched by northern lights. And what everyone thought was a tower, was actually a beanstalk! A foolish king lived in this castle, and owned all the land around it.
